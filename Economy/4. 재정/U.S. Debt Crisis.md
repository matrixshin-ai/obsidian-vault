There Is No Looming U.S. Debt Crisis 

America has unique advantages that allow it to borrow more money than other nations.  

That’s because, especially in times of uncertainty, there is rising global demand for dollars and dollar-denominated debt. That demand, in turn, is driven by the unique role the U.S. and the dollar play in international finance. 

The strong appetite for Treasuries means that the U.S. government can meet its debt servicing needs by issuing new long-dated securities that lock in a low interest rate for a decade or more.
